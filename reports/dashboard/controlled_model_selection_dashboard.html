Safety-routing evaluation

Confidence thresholds were selected only from validation data. The frozen test set was used once for final evaluation.

Safety conclusion

BioGPT agreement improved accuracy and reduced unsafe errors, but did not eliminate them. Therefore, model confidence and agreement are treated as review-prioritization signals, not authorization for autonomous clinical decisions.

Final human-in-the-loop workflow

1

Qwen decision

Produces an evidence-based yes, no, or maybe decision and rationale.

2

BioGPT verification

Provides an independent biomedical decision signal.

3

Safety checks

Records confidence, agreement, evidence grounding, and risk signals.

4

Human review

Reviews any clinical-facing decision, uncertainty, disagreement, or risk.

5

Final decision

A qualified reviewer remains responsible for the final conclusion.

Scope and limitations

  • This is a research-stage evidence-assistance framework, not a clinical deployment.
  • PubMedQA measures evidence-question decisions and is not a substitute for real clinical validation.
  • The controlled test set has 200 rows and cannot establish clinical safety.
  • Further evaluation is required across additional clinical, demographic, and expert-reviewed datasets.
